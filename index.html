01 — ABOUT THE LAB

Building a future where everyone can use technology with confidence.

SecurityPlus AI Lab explores how assistive and emerging technologies—including artificial intelligence—can safely and ethically support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD), and the people who support them.

Our work connects disability services research with practical technology education. We partner across families, schools, service providers, and research communities to make digital tools more accessible, useful, and safe throughout life.

03 — RESEARCH IN ACTION

Turning research
into everyday practice.

We study not only what technology can do, but how people learn to use it—and how that knowledge carries into daily support.

01

Technology training for supporters

Develop practical training for direct support professionals, teachers, families, and other staff—and study how they bring what they learn to the people they support.

02

Safe technology use across the lifespan

Create digital literacy and cybersecurity guidance that reflects different life stages, from childhood through older adulthood.

03

Ethical AI in disability services

Explore guidelines for privacy, consent, transparency, and bias when AI is used in support, education, and care.

05 — WHY IT MATTERS

Access to technology should never mean facing its risks alone.

People with IDD—and the families and professionals who support them—can have limited access to technology training while still facing real digital risks, including scams, loss of privacy, and misuse of AI.

SecurityPlus AI Lab works toward practical, accessible training so technology can strengthen independence, communication, and quality of life.
